# Continuity Ledger — Book One

This is the working continuity source of truth. Update it whenever a draft changes who knows what, who has met whom, where somebody is housed, or when an important event occurs.

## Calendar status

Draft V2 uses day/month headings without a year. Exact story year remains open until period-specific legal and HMP Lewes details are finalised.

**Locked timing rule:** Connor's arrest, police custody, court and prison arrival span several days. He is arrested late on a Friday and does not arrive at HMP Lewes until Monday.

## Wing status — creator-supplied period canon

These details are story canon for drafting but should be checked against the final story year before publication.

- **G Wing** — induction / early-arrival wing in the creator's remembered period.
- Connor physically lives on G Wing for roughly **two to three days**, even though the broader induction process may continue for longer.
- **C Wing** — double bang-up. Connor and Craig move here after the initial G Wing period. Connor shares a cell with Jamie "Jamo" Mills; Craig is housed elsewhere on C Wing.
- **A Wing** — double bang-up. Jay, Deano and Leon can be based here during the opening movement.
- **M Wing** — single bang-up. Mason can be based here while his prison job brings him through reception/induction areas.
- Wing locations are used to make the prison legible to the reader without turning the novel into an operational map.

## FRIDAY 14 OCTOBER — ARREST

### Connor
- Age: 21.
- At home after spending the day working his cannabis line.
- Believes he got away with the aggravated burglary because time has passed without arrest.
- No solicitor/case in his conscious life before arrest.
- Armed police arrive late in the evening.
- Connor realises arrest concerns aggravated burglary.
- Crime facts known to Connor: watches taken; victim's Mercedes C63 AMG taken; Connor noticed a cut afterwards but did not connect it with the crime.
- Connor enters police custody that night.
- He does **not** see Craig and does **not** reach HMP Lewes on Friday.

## SATURDAY 15 OCTOBER — POLICE CUSTODY

### Connor
- Still in police custody.
- Solicitor becomes part of Connor's case only after arrest.
- Solicitor tells him recovered C63 contains blood attributed to Connor.
- Connor realises the blood must relate to the cut on his middle finger.
- Connor is the only person identified by the available physical evidence.
- Witnesses allege a gun was present. No gun recovered. Connor does not admit one.
- Connor gives a no-comment interview and does not identify co-offenders.
- Police frame alleged firearm as evidence Connor “crossed the line.”
- Connor catastrophises about 10/12/15 years.
- Rhythm: cell, solicitor, interview, cell, waiting.

## SUNDAY 16 OCTOBER — WAITING

### Connor
- Still not at HMP Lewes.
- Poor sleep, uncertainty and nothing useful to do create obsessive thinking.
- Thinks repeatedly about mum, brothers, sister, little brother and girlfriend.
- Worries about co-offenders without identifying them.
- Blood evidence, alleged firearm and imagined sentence length loop through his head.
- The lack of control is the event; do not rush this day because it contains less outward action.

## MONDAY 17 OCTOBER — COURT / REMAND / LEWES / G WING

### Connor
- Court after weekend custody period.
- Decisive emotional beat: remanded and **not going home**.
- Prison transport to HMP Lewes.
- Sees ordinary outside life through custody transport and thinks about family and sentence length.
- HMP Lewes arrival/reception.
- Connor first sees Craig only in reception holding area.
- Connor hears: “Craig's here.”
- After reception Connor reaches **G Wing**, the early-arrival/induction wing.

### Craig
- Reaches/is processed through HMP Lewes in the same broad Monday arrival period.
- Facing major organised-crime case, working headline: large-scale conspiracy to supply Class A drugs; additional organised-crime/money-laundering counts possible.
- No Book One POV.
- Connor does not yet know Craig or that Connor's old cannabis line sat several levels beneath Craig's network.
- Craig also spends the early period on/through G Wing before later movement to C Wing.

## TUESDAY 18 OCTOBER — G WING / THE NAME

### Mason
- Experienced repeat prisoner and reception/induction peer-worker.
- Recognises Connor as an obvious frightened first-timer.
- Recognises Craig by reputation, not as a personal friend.
- Warns Connor not to collect other people's problems.

### Leah
- Sees Connor as inexperienced.
- Sees Craig as risk information and unusual interest rather than myth.
- No emotional attachment yet.

### Connor
- First full prison day after arrival.
- Attempts family contact; mum/family emotional anchor.
- First proper conversation with Craig.
- Craig seems unexpectedly ordinary compared with rumours.
- Connor does not yet understand Craig's scale.

### Malik
- Learns people outside Lewes are asking after Craig.
- May suspect interest but does not yet know confirmed money exists.

## WEDNESDAY 19 OCTOBER — G WING / THE MESSAGE

### Big Sharky
- Receives confirmation Craig is at Lewes.
- Older than Craig; old personal history remains hidden.
- Makes it known through contacts that serious money is attached to Craig.
- Reader does not learn exact feud origin.

### Malik
- Learns enough to realise the money is not ordinary prison gossip.
- Information becomes valuable and dangerous.

### Jay — A Wing
- Hears the message through his own friendship/outside network.
- Does not automatically want involvement.
- Resents outsiders treating prisoners as disposable people who will serve the consequences.

### Connor — G Wing
- Still primarily concerned with case/family/prison adjustment.
- Reader knows more about Craig danger than Connor.

### Bennett
- Separate prisoner-connected coercion storyline begins tightening.
- No operational contraband mechanics on page.

## THURSDAY 20 OCTOBER — MOVE TO C WING / EVERYBODY KNOWS SOMETHING

### Connor
- After roughly two to three days physically on G Wing, Connor is told he is moving to **C Wing**.
- Carries his property onto the main wing and immediately feels watched because he looks fresh.
- The move changes prison emotionally: G Wing felt temporary; C Wing feels like somewhere he may actually have to live.
- An alarm interrupts his first arrival on C Wing.
- Connor witnesses the **non-graphic aftermath** of a serious assault near the shower area. In the confusion he initially believes the injury is worse than it later proves to be.
- Later in the story he sees the injured prisoner healed, reinforcing that first impressions under fear can distort reality.
- Scene purpose: ordinary prison life can turn frightening in seconds, while experienced prisoners' ability to resume normal conversation is almost as shocking to Connor as the incident itself.

### Connor / Craig
- Craig moves to C Wing in the same broad period but is **not** Connor's cellmate.
- Their proximity becomes increasingly visible to other prisoners.

### Jamie "Jamo" Mills — C Wing
- Connor's new cellmate.
- Also in prison for the first time, but roughly six months into his sentence/remand experience.
- Knows enough to look experienced to Connor while still being a novice beside Mason and older repeat prisoners.
- Gives Connor an informal, funny, imperfect breakdown of who is who.
- Jamo's knowledge is never omniscient: some information is true, some rumour, some incomplete.

### Social map Connor begins learning
- **C Wing:** Connor, Craig, Jamo; Malik can be based here; Bruce/Macca/Darren can appear here regularly.
- **A Wing:** Jay, Deano, Leon during the opening movement.
- **M Wing:** Mason, with a single cell; his prison job takes him through reception/induction areas.
- Exact secondary placements can move later if story mechanics require, but the reader should always know the broad wing context of a scene.

### Jamo's who-is-who function
- Mason — old hand / institutional wisdom.
- Bruce — underestimated wildcard who somehow knows everyone.
- Malik — information broker; useful but dangerous to overshare with.
- Jay — connected, socially confident, dealing with outside loyalties.
- Deano — separate circle, wants other people's feuds kept away from his people.
- Leon — routine/gym-focused, avoids politics.
- Darren — dependency/recovery world.
- Macca — family-focused; prison measured in calls and visits.
- Craig — stories far exceed what Jamo can actually verify.

### General information state
- Craig money story mutating across wings.
- Different prisoners have different figures/versions.
- Some treat it as gossip, some as opportunity, some as danger.
- Nobody should have perfect information.

## SATURDAY 22 OCTOBER — PRESSURE

### Connor — C Wing
- Seen near Craig often enough for others to notice.
- Does not yet understand that proximity itself can create reputation/risk.

### Mason
- Warns Connor about borrowed reputation / other people's problems.
- Gives Connor the prison advice: **“Hear all. See all. Say nothing.”**
- Meaning: notice everything, but do not prove what you know, repeat every story or place yourself in every dispute.
- This becomes a recurring Connor principle and later a test he sometimes passes and sometimes ignores.

### Bruce
- Senses something approaching before Connor does.

### Leah
- Notices Connor drifting into Craig's orbit.
- Still professional; no romantic framing.

### Big Sharky
- Patient, personal, remote pressure continues.

## SUNDAY 23 OCTOBER — THE CHOICE

### Threat event
- Trouble reaches Craig in an ordinary prison/main-wing context.
- Scene is brief, tense and non-graphic.
- No instructional planning mechanics.

### Connor
- Has only seconds to choose.
- Instinctively backs Craig rather than stepping away.
- Consistent with prior loyalty: no-comment interview, refusal to identify co-offenders, family protectiveness.
- Action publicly witnessed.
- Prison identity changes immediately.

### Craig
- Comes through event.
- Begins treating Connor as someone who proved himself.
- No grand gratitude speech.

### Leah/staff
- Staff version differs from prisoner mythology forming immediately afterward.

### End-of-opening status
- Connor: unknown first-timer → publicly associated with Craig.
- Craig: reputation + active outside threat confirmed.
- Reader understands more than Connor about Big Sharky but still not why feud exists.
- Middle of Book One begins from changed social order.

## Hidden truths reserved for later

- Connor's cannabis line ultimately sat several layers beneath Craig's wider network. Connor does not know this during opening week.
- Craig likes Connor partly because he recognises his younger self in him. This is inferred from behaviour in Book One, not stated through Craig POV.
- Big Sharky believes Craig did him dirty/set him up years earlier. That belief is wrong or incomplete.
- Full Craig/Big Sharky history is Book Two material.
- Connor ultimately receives five years, but neither Connor nor reader should feel certain of that during opening remand period.
- Connor's girlfriend later cheats while he is imprisoned; do not rush this subplot into opening week.
