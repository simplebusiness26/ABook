# Title Canon

## Book One

**Canonical title:** *Hear All, See All, Say Nothing*

This title is locked for the current Book One project.

Use it on:
- manuscript title pages;
- phone-friendly reading copies;
- PDFs and DOCX exports;
- beta-reader editions;
- future cover concepts;
- publication metadata when the book reaches that stage.

Older references to *Big Shark in a Creek* are superseded. They can remain in historical drafts until those files are next revised, but any newly generated or regenerated document must use **Hear All, See All, Say Nothing**.

The phrase also exists inside the novel as Mason's advice to Connor: **“Hear all. See all. Say nothing.”** Its meaning should deepen as Connor's prison identity develops.
