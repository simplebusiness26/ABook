# Third-party audio components

The zero-cost pipeline is configured for:

- Kokoro inference library: <https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro>
- Kokoro-82M model and built-in voice packs: <https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M>
- FFmpeg: <https://ffmpeg.org/>
- eSpeak NG fallback pronunciation engine: <https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng>

Kokoro's repository and model card currently identify the model/code as permissively licensed. FFmpeg and eSpeak NG have their own licence conditions. Recheck the upstream licences and the distribution rules of the chosen publishing platform immediately before commercial release; this file records the components but is not legal advice.

No cloned celebrity, actor, narrator or private person's voice is used by this configuration. Only built-in model voice packs are selected.
