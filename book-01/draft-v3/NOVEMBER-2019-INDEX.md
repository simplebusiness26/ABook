# November 2019 — Draft V3 Index

**Status: NOVEMBER IN PROGRESS.**

This month is the transition into the full ensemble middle of Book One. Craig remains important, but the prison increasingly carries whole chapters through other people.

## Chapters drafted

- `27-01-NOVEMBER-2019.md` — **THE PERSON YOU SAID YOU WERE**
  - Tyler's invented cousin reputation produces a real expectation.
  - Connor recognises Tyler making the same sort of ego-driven mistake Connor made with Kez.
  - Tyler's mum punctures the prison persona by calling him Ty and asking ordinary caring questions.
  - Josh's love of cars becomes part of his energetic identity.
  - Connor/Jamo tension continues rather than exploding immediately.
  - Officer Mercer notices newer prisoners beginning to look to Connor for guidance.

- `28-03-NOVEMBER-2019.md` — **ROOM FOR TWO**
  - Connor/Jamo finally have the argument the whole of October has been building toward.
  - Jamo admits jealousy without allowing Connor to use it to erase his legitimate grievance.
  - Jamo reveals he applied to move before the argument.
  - The prison abruptly grants the move because a bed becomes available.
  - Connor gets the single cell he thinks he wants and discovers the quiet is not entirely a reward.
  - Darren/Josh continue to become an ordinary, affectionate pair in the background.

- `29-05-NOVEMBER-2019.md` — **STUART**
  - Stuart is allocated into Connor's cell after Jamo leaves.
  - His self-defence account, university background and Christian faith are introduced without graphic detail.
  - Stuart immediately insists on calling him Connor rather than Rush.
  - Their humour begins alongside the moral/spiritual contrast.
  - Connor and Jamo have their first awkward post-cellmate interaction, showing the friendship is damaged rather than destroyed.
  - Officer Mercer observes how cell allocations quietly reshape sentences.

- `30-08-NOVEMBER-2019.md` — **SMALL THINGS**
  - Officer Grant pressures Darren again.
  - Darren gives a small piece of information he immediately regrets.
  - A later staff intervention around Jinx's wider circle may or may not have resulted from Darren's words.
  - Darren is trapped by not knowing whether he caused it.
  - Malik notices the timing but deliberately resists immediately spreading an accusation.
  - Jinx and Deano recognise that suspicion of informing is becoming its own threat.
  - Josh/Darren/Bruce keep the chapter human and funny through Josh's car obsession.

- `31-10-NOVEMBER-2019.md` — **LAYERS**
  - Connor casually mentions an outside name Craig recognises.
  - Craig reveals that Connor's old cannabis world sat inside a much larger network Connor could not see.
  - The key lesson is that Connor "ran the bit he could see" rather than genuinely understanding the whole system around him.
  - Craig refuses to let Connor romanticise the discovery into proof that he was secretly important.
  - Stuart reframes the revelation morally rather than criminally.
  - Malik notices Connor learning to keep information to himself.
  - Bruce explains organised crime through a damaged onion metaphor because Bruce remains Bruce.

- `32-12-NOVEMBER-2019.md` — **WHAT PEOPLE KNOW**
  - Officer Bennett's coercion/fear becomes visibly harder to hide.
  - Senior Officer Patel and Officer Mercer both begin noticing behavioural changes.
  - Tyler's fake reputation tightens when the prisoner who believed him comes back expecting results.
  - Bruce gives Tyler bizarre but accurate advice.
  - Connor gives Tyler the lesson he learned from Kez: stop creating a new lie to protect the last one.
  - Tyler manages his first small honest admission to his mum: prison is not always easy.
  - Craig independently notices Bennett's fear.
  - Officer Mercer notices Craig noticing Bennett, deepening her dangerous curiosity about what Craig sees.

## Next November chapters

The next run should continue from 12 November with:

1. **Tyler finally admitting the cousin story is exaggerated** and dealing with the social embarrassment rather than solving it through a bigger performance.
2. **Jamo's life away from Connor's cell** — show that moving out does not magically fix his sentence and begin creating space for eventual reconciliation.
3. **Stuart/Connor warmth** — faith, family and ordinary cell life before Stuart's later deterioration arc begins much farther ahead.
4. **Darren/Grant consequences** — Darren becomes more frightened that his words affected Jinx's circle; Malik chooses not to spread a dangerous suspicion without proof.
5. **Yusuf's loyalty test begins** when two people he likes start expecting incompatible social loyalty.
6. **Macca family interruption** — a wing/staff event interferes with contact from home at precisely the wrong time.
7. **Officer Mercer / Officer Bennett mirror deepens** without rushing either officer into a major boundary breach yet.
8. **Craig can disappear for stretches** so November genuinely proves that prison, not Craig alone, is carrying the novel.

## November emotional target

By the end of November:

- Connor should be more comfortable as Rush but more challenged by Stuart's use of his real name;
- Jamo and Connor should miss each other before either is willing to admit it;
- Darren and Josh should still primarily feel warm and funny together;
- Tyler should become more sympathetic as the reader sees how fear built the persona;
- Malik should begin understanding that information can destroy as easily as empower;
- Officer Mercer should be increasingly emotionally curious about Craig while still outwardly professional;
- Officer Bennett should feel prison following him home;
- the ensemble should be strong enough that a whole chapter can pass with Craig barely appearing.
