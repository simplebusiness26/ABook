# October 2019 — Draft V3 Index

**Status: OCTOBER MANUSCRIPT PASS COMPLETE.**

October is written as selected dated chapters rather than one chapter for every calendar day. The gaps are intentional: ordinary time passes between scenes, while the manuscript lands on the days where character relationships, prison routine or plot materially change.

## October chapters

- `12-01-OCTOBER-2019.md` — **THE NAME STICKS**
  - Rush becomes a social identity.
  - First tiny Jamo resentment.
  - Darren/Grant pressure opens.
  - Malik/Yusuf and Leah observe Connor changing.

- `13-02-OCTOBER-2019.md` — **USEFUL**
  - Kez pressures Connor to attend a separate visit with an unknown woman connected to Kez's people.
  - Connor is expected to take responsibility for a prohibited parcel and return from the visit with it.
  - Connor hears being used as being respected.
  - Jamo spots the danger.
  - Darren gives Grant information he tells himself is harmless.

- `14-03-OCTOBER-2019.md` — **HOME**
  - Connor has a genuine family visit with his mum and girlfriend.
  - The visit carries the emotional cost of seeing home continue without him.
  - His little brother sleeping in Connor's T-shirt becomes an important family beat.
  - Jamo knows the separate Kez visit is still hanging over Connor.

- `15-04-OCTOBER-2019.md` — **IMPORTANT**
  - Kez confirms the pressured visit is happening next day.
  - Connor begins wondering why the people praising him are not carrying the risk themselves.
  - Jamo's trusted-job application continues stalling.
  - Mason deepens the title advice.
  - Darren gives Grant another supposedly harmless fragment.

- `15A-05-OCTOBER-2019.md` — **THE OTHER VISIT**
  - Connor sits a visit with a woman he has never met.
  - She passes responsibility for a prohibited parcel to him.
  - Connor then has to face the normal post-visit search process, including a strip search, while terrified of being caught.
  - The scene makes the act and the risk clear without describing concealment or search-evasion mechanics.
  - Connor gets through undetected, initially mistakes survival for proof of courage, and returns visibly shaken.
  - Jamo realises Connor has done something serious and worries that Connor's risks are becoming his too.
  - Craig delivers the key lesson: **useful is not the same as important**.
  - Kez's casual reaction afterwards proves Craig's point.

- `16-06-OCTOBER-2019.md` — **THE SPIN**
  - Connor/Jamo's cell is searched the day after the pressured visit.
  - Nothing dramatic is found; the emotional/institutional consequences matter more.
  - Jamo's frustration becomes legitimate rather than simple jealousy.
  - Leah reminds Connor that trusted work and unnecessary problems do not mix.

- `17-08-OCTOBER-2019.md` — **WING CLEANER**
  - Connor learns his reputation/security attention closes trusted-job options.
  - He is allocated wing cleaning.
  - Bruce attempts to rename him Mopfather.
  - Leon provides the contrast: discipline/routine earned him gym orderly.

- `18-10-OCTOBER-2019.md` — **DARREN**
  - Darren becomes a full character rather than background addiction.
  - Grant's questioning becomes more deliberate.
  - Connor learns Grant has been asking around Kez/Connor's circle.
  - Malik begins noticing Darren's behaviour without yet declaring him an informer.

- `19-12-OCTOBER-2019.md` — **OTHER PEOPLE'S DAYS**
  - One lockdown becomes multiple different prisons.
  - Macca loses a family call opportunity.
  - Leon loses gym/routine.
  - Jinx/Deano politics continue.
  - Bennett's fear follows him outside the gate.
  - Connor starts understanding that one institutional decision can hit ten lives differently.

- `20-14-OCTOBER-2019.md` — **JOSH**
  - Josh arrives energetic, funny, restless and curious.
  - Vehicle-taking offence direction is introduced naturally.
  - Darren becomes his older guide.
  - Josh meets Connor, Bruce and Macca.
  - The Darren/Josh relationship begins as genuinely good for both of them.

- `21-17-OCTOBER-2019.md` — **SOMEBODY KNOWS SOMETHING**
  - Darren/Josh become warmer and funnier together.
  - Grant's information pressure tightens.
  - Jamo's visits-orderly application is explicitly delayed by association/security concerns.
  - Darren begins feeling protective of Josh.

- `22-20-OCTOBER-2019.md` — **OUTSIDE**
  - Connor speaks to his little brother and feels the powerlessness of prison.
  - First meaningful crack with his girlfriend.
  - Macca articulates prison as absence.
  - Josh/Darren/Bruce provide warmth against Connor's outside anxiety.

- `23-22-OCTOBER-2019.md` — **TYLER**
  - Tyler arrives frightened and immediately performs toughness.
  - His cousin/reputation claims begin.
  - Josh is delighted not to be the newest prisoner.
  - Tyler's call to his mum shows the frightened boy beneath the performance.
  - Bruce recognises the fear without humiliating him for it.

- `24-25-OCTOBER-2019.md` — **STORIES**
  - Tyler's invented history expands because people listen.
  - Bennett's coercion becomes part of his life outside work.
  - Leah notices Bennett and finds herself increasingly attentive to Craig.
  - Grant and Darren's relationship moves closer to explicit informing pressure.

- `25-28-OCTOBER-2019.md` — **KNOCKED BACK**
  - Jamo loses the current visits-orderly opportunity.
  - His resentment toward Connor becomes both unfair and understandable.
  - Mason stops him letting an unfair decision choose the rest of his sentence.
  - Darren/Josh are now an established pair.
  - Jinx and Deano form a reluctant practical understanding.
  - Leah becomes certain something is wrong with Bennett.

- `26-31-OCTOBER-2019.md` — **ONE MONTH IN**
  - Connor recognises how rapidly Lewes has become normal.
  - Jamo/Connor remain friends but the cell relationship is cracking.
  - Josh is happy, energetic and fully attached to Darren.
  - Tyler's false reputation produces its first real demand.
  - Darren pushes back against Grant because Josh now gives him a reason to protect something.
  - Leah carries thoughts of Craig outside work and sees Bennett deteriorating.
  - Jinx/Deano's reluctant cooperation deepens.
  - Macca receives his daughter's drawing.
  - The month closes with Connor comfortable enough inside prison for that comfort itself to become worrying.

## October arc achieved

October begins with Connor enjoying being **Rush**.

It ends with the reward already producing costs:

- status among prisoners / distrust from the institution;
- Craig's attention / other prisoners trying to use him;
- a new identity / a strained friendship with Jamo;
- greater prison confidence / increasing distance from the outside world.

At the same time the ensemble has expanded so November no longer depends on Connor/Craig to carry every chapter.
