# October 2019 — Start Note

## Do we already have what we need?

**Yes.** There is enough locked canon to start writing October without stopping for more broad planning.

Already available:

- Connor / Rush transformation and the Craig relationship;
- Jamo's education / visits-orderly ambition and the reasons jealousy can later become real resentment;
- Darren's addiction + informing pressure from Officer Grant;
- Josh's personality, vehicle-related offence direction and eventual relationship with Darren;
- Tyler's fear / false-reputation arc;
- Connor's eventual wing-cleaner job and why trusted jobs are closed to him;
- Stuart's later arrival as Connor's cellmate;
- Leah / Bennett parallel corruption arcs;
- Jinx / Deano / Malik / Mason / Leon / Macca / Yusuf / Bruce ensemble functions;
- 2019 prison-life canon: IEP, canteen, jobs, education, searches, paperwork, visits, laundry, gym lists, applications, property and ordinary wing friction;
- Connor and Craig's legal timelines and the February 2020 double-departure ending.

Small details such as exact surnames, Josh's final age, Tyler's exact charge or the exact mundane reason for a future court adjournment can be locked when the relevant scene needs them. They do **not** need to hold up the manuscript.

## What October must accomplish

October is the month where **Rush starts to feel good before it starts costing Connor anything serious**.

Core movement:

1. The nickname sticks and Connor notices that people treat him differently.
2. Jamo is still Connor's mate, but the first imbalance enters the cell.
3. Darren becomes a full person before Josh arrives.
4. Grant starts exploiting Darren's vulnerability for information.
5. Connor is pulled into a risky visit-related prohibited favour because another group sees him as eager and useful.
6. The visit scene is written around fear, pressure and being used — never operational smuggling detail.
7. Craig corrects Connor's idea of status: being willing to carry another man's risk does not make Connor important.
8. Connor's association / reputation begins closing trusted-job options.
9. A search / disciplinary consequence damages Jamo's path toward visits orderly and gives him a legitimate reason to resent the chaos around Connor.
10. Connor ends up as a wing cleaner.
11. Josh arrives while still funny, energetic, restless and easy to love.
12. Darren naturally takes Josh under his wing.
13. Tyler arrives later as another young first-timer whose fear presents very differently.

October should end with several warm relationships established **before** November begins pulling them apart.

# What comes immediately after October

## November 2019 — Relationships start changing shape

- Jamo and Connor's friendship deteriorates properly rather than instantly.
- Jamo eventually moves out.
- Stuart becomes Connor's new cellmate.
- Connor and Stuart build an unlikely friendship around faith, righteousness and Connor's complete refusal to admit he is listening.
- Darren and Josh become an established, funny older-brother / younger-brother pair.
- Grant's pressure on Darren gets harder to dismiss.
- Malik begins noticing that somebody is feeding information.
- Jinx and Deano are forced into cooperation around consequences they did not create.
- Connor starts learning the scale of Craig's world.

## December 2019 — Christmas / belonging / absence

Christmas becomes a major ensemble movement, not a montage.

- Macca's family story should hurt.
- Connor's girlfriend relationship worsens while family remains emotionally central.
- Josh should still be recognisably Josh and fun to be around.
- Darren's influence on him is becoming normal rather than obviously disastrous.
- Stuart's spiritual worldview gets further under Connor's skin.
- Leah's attachment to Craig deepens.
- Bennett becomes increasingly trapped.
- Bruce, Yusuf, Leon and everyday prison absurdity keep the book warm and funny between darker material.

## January 2020 — Consequences

- Connor expects sentence and gets knocked back / adjourned again.
- Craig spends more time at court as his contested case intensifies.
- Connor overreaches by treating Craig's reputation as if it belongs to him.
- Connor pushes Stuart out of the cell.
- Stuart deteriorates elsewhere before eventually winning his self-defence case and leaving Lewes.
- Tyler's invented persona finally creates consequences he cannot bluff through.
- Leah recognises Bennett's situation and tries to stop it becoming irreversible.
- Josh's deterioration becomes visible enough that people start commenting on it.

## Late January / February 2020 — Payoff and departure

- Josh dies following a substance-related medical collapse, handled through aftermath rather than graphic detail.
- Darren survives and has to live with the knowledge that he helped make that world normal to Josh.
- Malik learns when information has to stop with him.
- Jinx chooses the people actually around him over demands from outside.
- Connor receives five years and Cat C allocation.
- Craig receives his major sentence and dispersal / high-security allocation.
- Connor faces a final choice that echoes the original Craig attack, except this time he **pauses and chooses** rather than simply rushing.
- Connor and Craig share their final Lewes conversation.
- Their respective transports take them into two different Book Two worlds.

## Writing rule from here

Stop treating the outline as the story.

From this point onward, write scenes that make the reader **live inside Lewes**: funny conversations, jobs, canteen, cells, visits, arguments, waiting, boredom, rumours, ordinary friendships and sudden collisions between completely unrelated lives.

The major plot should keep arriving through ordinary prison days rather than replacing them.
