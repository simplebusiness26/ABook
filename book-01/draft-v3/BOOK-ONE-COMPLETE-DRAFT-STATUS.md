# Book One Draft V3 — Complete Draft Status

**Title:** *Hear All, See All, Say Nothing*

**Status:** COMPLETE STORY DRAFT FOR AUTHOR REVIEW.

**Story span:** Friday 20 September 2019 to Tuesday 25 February 2020.

The canonical Draft V3 now runs from Connor's arrest, police custody and first remand arrival at HMP Lewes through the complete Lewes ensemble arc, Christmas, January consequences, Josh's death, Connor and Craig's sentences, and their separate departures into different prison worlds.

## Completion state

The draft now contains the full planned Book One movement:

- Connor is arrested for the aggravated burglary and learns the blood in the recovered Mercedes links him to the offence.
- He gives a no-comment interview and is remanded to HMP Lewes.
- Connor and Craig arrive at Lewes at roughly the same story time for completely different legal reasons.
- Connor moves G Wing to C Wing and meets Jamo.
- Big Sharky's remote pressure reaches Lewes and the failed attack on Craig changes Connor's prison identity.
- Bruce names Connor **Rush**.
- Connor enjoys the status, gets used by Kez, and learns the difference between being important and being useful.
- Connor's risky choices damage Jamo's cell life and trusted-job ambitions; their friendship fractures and later rebuilds.
- Connor becomes a wing cleaner while his prisoner status rises and his institutional trust falls.
- Darren is established as a complete character before Josh arrives.
- Darren and Josh build a genuine warm older-mate/younger-mate friendship.
- Tyler arrives, invents a tougher reputation, gets trapped by it, and eventually admits how frightened he has been.
- Stuart becomes Connor's cellmate, challenges Rush morally, is later pushed out by Connor, deteriorates under remand pressure, wins his self-defence case and leaves legally cleared but changed.
- Malik learns that information can be responsibility rather than currency.
- Jinx and Deano move from suspicion toward practical cooperation; Jinx ultimately chooses the people physically around him over outside demands.
- Yusuf refuses to become everybody's messenger simply because everybody likes him.
- Leon learns that neutrality and routine cannot protect him from every moral choice.
- Macca's family story makes absence, missed time and ordinary love a major prison experience independent of Craig.
- Officer Bennett's coercion worsens until he finally begins telling the truth.
- Officer Mercer moves from professional curiosity about Craig into emotional compromise; Craig eventually learns her private first name, **Leah**, as a deliberate boundary beat.
- Craig's influence helps remove the immediate outside pressure around Bennett without operational mechanics being shown.
- Josh suffers a non-graphic substance-related medical collapse and dies after hospital transfer.
- Darren survives and carries the guilt; the death does not magically cure him.
- Connor receives **five years** and is allocated toward the Category C estate.
- Craig receives a working-draft sentence of **twenty-two years** and is allocated toward the high-security/dispersal estate.
- Connor's final test echoes the September attack: this time he pauses, understands what he is seeing and chooses rather than simply reacting.
- Connor and Craig have a final Lewes conversation. Craig still calls him Connor because he knows his name.
- Connor and Craig leave Lewes through separate transport processes for separate prison worlds.
- The final scene returns to Mason at induction as Lewes begins again with another frightened new arrival.
