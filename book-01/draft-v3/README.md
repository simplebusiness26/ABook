- clearly labelled POV sections;
- Connor is the anchor, not the only important character;
- Craig receives no POV in Book One;
- Big Sharky receives no interior POV in Book One;
- everyday prison life must remain visible between dramatic beats;
- criminality/contraband can shape plot but is not written as practical instruction;
- violence is vivid enough to follow but non-graphic;
- humour, boredom, family life and institutional frustration are part of the story, not filler.
