# Book One — Active Draft V2

This folder is the **active manuscript direction** for Book One.

The older files in `book-01/chapters/` and any superseded dated Draft V2 files remain useful source material, but the continuity ledger and current structure files override them where chronology conflicts.

## Locked opening chronology

Connor's arrest, custody, court and prison arrival must take several days.

1. **Friday 14 October — ARREST** — home → armed police → first police cell.
2. **Saturday 15 October — CUSTODY** — solicitor → recovered C63/blood evidence → remembered middle-finger cut → alleged firearm seriousness → no-comment interview → more waiting.
3. **Sunday 16 October — WAITING** — custody, poor sleep, uncertainty, family thoughts and sentence panic; Connor is not yet in Lewes.
4. **Monday 17 October — LEWES / G WING** — court → remand → prison transport → HMP Lewes reception → first sight of Craig → G Wing.
5. **Tuesday 18 October — G WING** — first full prison day; Mason/Leah/Connor/Malik perspectives begin widening the world.
6. **Wednesday 19 October — G WING / THE MESSAGE** — Big Sharky learns Craig is at Lewes and serious money becomes attached to him.
7. **Thursday 20 October — MOVE TO C WING** — Connor and Craig leave the induction wing for C Wing; Connor gets his first proper cellmate and begins learning the prison's social map.

## Wing story map

Creator-supplied period detail is now recorded in `bible/LEWES_WING_STORY_MAP.md`.

These are treated as creator-supplied period details until the exact story year is locked and checked.

## New C Wing chapter

`04-20-OCTOBER.md` is the active draft for Connor's move from G Wing to C Wing.

It introduces:
- the emotional difference between induction and a main wing;
- Connor arriving with his property while experienced prisoners immediately recognise him as fresh;
- a sudden alarm and the non-graphic aftermath of a serious assault near the shower area, giving Connor a frightening first impression of main-wing life;
- the later understanding that what Connor thought he saw in the confusion was worse than the actual injury;
- Jamie "Jamo" Mills, Connor's C Wing cellmate, who is also a first-time prisoner but has been inside around six months;
- Jamo informally giving Connor the first proper "who's who" tour of the recurring cast;
- clearer wing locations for the ensemble;
- the rule that Jamo's prison knowledge is useful but not omniscient: rumours, truth and reputation remain different things.

An older `04-17-OCTOBER.md` file is now superseded as chronology/scene structure by `04-20-OCTOBER.md`; useful material from it can still be mined during later editing.

## Where the manuscript is heading

The middle movement develops:
- Connor becoming informally close to Craig;
- Connor enjoying status and then discovering its cost;
- Connor's cellmate watching that transformation from close range;
- Leah's professional curiosity becoming emotionally dangerous;
- Bennett's worsening coercion/corruption arc;
- Jay and Deano dealing with fallout from the Craig threat;
- Malik learning more than is safe;
- Darren, Macca, Leon, Bruce, Mason and others showing different versions of prison;
- Connor's girlfriend/outside relationship deteriorating;
- Connor eventually overreaching by acting as though Craig's reputation belongs to him;
- Craig's case moving toward sentence and eventual transfer into the dispersal system.

Craig remains **non-POV** throughout Book One.

Big Sharky's true history with Craig remains hidden until later.
