## Where the manuscript now stands

The opening engine is drafted far enough to react to actual scenes instead of an outline.

Connor is now:
- in HMP Lewes;
- publicly associated with Craig;
- aware that the attack was not random;
- beginning to enjoy being known;
- aware that his supposedly independent outside drug line was part of a much larger structure;
- developing a genuine relationship with Craig;
- still uncertain about his own sentence and frightened about losing his family life.

Craig remains **non-POV**.

Big Sharky's true history with Craig remains hidden.

The next drafting phase is the **middle movement**: status, consequences, family deterioration, Leah's emotional boundary, Bennett's worsening compromise, Jay/Deano fallout, Connor becoming Craig's informal right-hand companion, Connor eventually misusing borrowed reputation, Craig's case moving toward sentence, and the road toward dispersal.
