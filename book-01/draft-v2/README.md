# Book One — Active Draft V2

This folder is now the **active manuscript direction** for Book One.

The older files in `book-01/chapters/` are preserved as source material/earlier drafts while the new dated mosaic structure is developed. Do not treat the old one-POV-per-chapter sequence as current canon where it conflicts with Draft V2.

## Important chronology correction

The first Draft V2 run compressed Connor's arrest, custody, court and HMP Lewes arrival too tightly.

**Canon now overrides those compressed dates:** Connor is arrested late on a **Friday** and does **not** arrive at HMP Lewes until **Monday**, after a weekend dominated by police custody, solicitor/interview stages, waiting, uncertainty and obsessive thinking.

The canonical opening is now:

1. **Friday 14 October — ARREST** — home → armed police → first police cell.
2. **Saturday 15 October — CUSTODY** — solicitor → recovered C63/blood evidence → remembered middle-finger cut → alleged firearm seriousness → no-comment interview → more waiting.
3. **Sunday 16 October — WAITING** — custody, uncertainty, family thoughts, sentence panic; Connor is not yet in Lewes.
4. **Monday 17 October — LEWES** — court → remand → prison transport → HMP Lewes reception → first sight of Craig.
5. **Tuesday 18 October onward** — the multi-POV prison mosaic expands and Craig's arrival begins travelling through the prison/outside networks.

The existing Draft V2 scene files remain valuable **scene material**, but their filename dates and compressed sequence should not be treated as final chronology until they are split/reordered in the next manuscript pass. `book-01/STRUCTURE_V2.md` is the current chronology authority.

## Where the manuscript now stands

The opening engine is drafted far enough to react to actual scenes instead of an outline, but the opening chronology is now being given **more breathing room**.

Connor is moving toward:
- HMP Lewes after several days of custody rather than a same-day transition;
- public association with Craig;
- awareness that the attack was not random;
- beginning to enjoy being known;
- learning that his supposedly independent outside drug line was part of a much larger structure;
- developing a genuine relationship with Craig;
- remaining uncertain about his own sentence and frightened about losing his family life.

Craig remains **non-POV**.

Big Sharky's true history with Craig remains hidden.

The next full manuscript pass should first split the current opening material into the Friday/Saturday/Sunday/Monday sequence, then carry the existing prison mosaic forward from Tuesday onward.
